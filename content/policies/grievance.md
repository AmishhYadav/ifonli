## Our commitments

- We **acknowledge** your complaint within **48 hours** of receiving it.
- We **resolve** it within **one month** of receiving it.
- Complaints are free of charge.

## How to file a complaint

You can reach us in any of these ways:

1. **Email** {{settings.grievance_officer.email}}.
2. **Phone** {{settings.grievance_officer.phone}} ({{settings.support_hours}}).
3. **Contact form** on our [Help page](/help).

Please include:

- your name, and the email and phone number you used for your order;
- your order reference, if your complaint is about an order;
- what went wrong and what you would like us to do;
- photos or documents, if they help.

If your order arrived damaged, defective, wrong or incomplete, the quickest route is **Report a problem** on your order page, within {{settings.damaged_claim_window_hours}} hours of delivery. See our [Returns and Refunds Policy](/policies/returns-refunds). If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you can bring it to the Grievance Officer here.

## What happens next

1. We note your complaint and send you an acknowledgement with a reference. This is within 48 hours.
2. We look into it and may ask you for more information.
3. We tell you our decision and what we will do about it, within one month.
4. If you disagree, tell us why. We will look again.

## If you are still not satisfied

You do not have to wait for us. You can approach these government channels at any time.

- **National Consumer Helpline (NCH):** call the toll-free number **1915**, message on WhatsApp or call **+91 8800001915**, or register a grievance at [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in). Operated by the Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India. Check the site for current hours.
- **Consumer Commissions:** you can file a consumer complaint online through [e-Jagriti](https://e-jagriti.gov.in), the Government of India's platform for consumer complaints. It has replaced the earlier e-Daakhil portal. Consumer Commissions hear complaints under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

For complaints about how your personal data is handled, see our [Privacy Notice](/policies/privacy).
