# Cancellation Policy

Changed your mind? No worries! Here's how cancellations work at {{settings.brand_name}}.

## Order Cancellation

Customers can request cancellation within **12 hours** of placing an order, provided the order has not been processed or dispatched.

## How to Cancel an Order

To cancel an order, customers can:

- Log in to their account and visit the My Orders section.
- Select the order they wish to cancel, if the cancellation option is available.
- Alternatively, contact us at {{settings.support_email}} or **[8828472665]**.

## Orders Already Dispatched

Orders that have already been dispatched cannot be cancelled through the standard cancellation process.

## Refund for Cancelled Orders

**Look First, Love Later!** 💖

We believe you should love what you receive! That's why we offer a special *Open Before You Accept* experience.

- *Unbox before you decide:* You can open your parcel at the time of delivery and take a look at your purchase before accepting it.
- *Love it? Keep it!* If everything looks good, accept your parcel and enjoy your new favourite find.
- *Not feeling it?* If the product isn't what you expected, you can decline the delivery at your doorstep, subject to the courier's open-box delivery process.

## Our Final Sale Policy

Once an order has been accepted and delivery is completed, we do not offer returns, exchanges or refunds for change of mind or personal preference.

We encourage you to make the most of our Look First, Love Later experience before accepting your parcel.

For products that arrive damaged, defective or incorrect, please contact our support team so we can assist you in accordance with applicable consumer rights.

## Cancellation by Us

We reserve the right to cancel orders due to stock unavailability, pricing errors, payment issues or suspected fraudulent activity. Customers will be informed, and eligible payments will be refunded.
