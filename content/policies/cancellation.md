# Cancellation Policy

This page explains when you can cancel an order, how to do it and how you get your money back. It also explains when we may cancel an order ourselves.

## When you can cancel

You can cancel an order yourself while its fulfilment status is: **{{settings.cancellable_statuses}}**.

- Your order page shows a **Cancel order** button only while cancellation is possible.
- Once your order has moved past that stage (for example, it has been packed or handed to the courier), you can no longer cancel it online. Contact us anyway and we will see what we can do. If we cannot stop the parcel, you can still use our [Returns and Refunds Policy](/policies/returns-refunds) once it arrives.
- Cancellation applies to the whole order. If you want to remove only one item, contact us and we will try to help.

## How to cancel

1. **From your order page.** Open your order from the link in your confirmation email, from the [Track order](/track) page, or from your account if you have one. Choose **Cancel order**.
2. **By contacting us.** Write to {{settings.support_email}} or use the [Help page](/help). Please include your order reference. You can also call {{settings.support_phone}} ({{settings.support_hours}}).

You will get an email confirming the cancellation.

## Prepaid orders (UPI, card, net banking, wallet)

- When you cancel a prepaid order in time, the refund starts automatically. You do not need to ask for it.
- You are refunded the full amount you paid for the cancelled order, to the **same payment method** you used.
- We start the refund within {{settings.policy.refund_initiation_days}} working days. Your bank or wallet may then take {{settings.policy.refund_bank_days}} to show it in your account.
- We email you when the refund is started and again when it is completed.
- The coupon used on a cancelled order is released, so you can use it again if it is still valid.

## Cash on Delivery orders

- You have not paid anything yet, so there is nothing to refund.
- Cancel the same way, from your order page or by contacting us.
- If the parcel is already out for delivery or has been handed to the courier, please contact us.

## When we cancel an order

We may need to cancel an order. For example:

- An item is out of stock. This can happen if an item sells out while a payment is still being completed.
- Your payment arrives after the stock hold on your cart has ended, and the item is no longer available.
- We cannot deliver to your address or pincode.
- A product was listed at an obviously wrong price because of an error.
- We have a genuine reason to believe an order is fraudulent or the payment is not valid.

If we cancel an order:

- We tell you by email as soon as we can, with the reason.
- If you have already paid, you get a **full refund** to your original payment method, started automatically. The timelines above apply.
- You are never charged for an order we cancel.

## Duplicate or unexpected payments

If you are charged twice for the same order, or money is taken but no order is created, we refund the extra or unmatched amount to your original payment method. If you do not see it, contact us with your payment reference. See also the answer in our [Help page](/help) for "payment failed but money was deducted".
